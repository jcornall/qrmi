.. _qrmi_overview:

QRMI Overview Paper
-------------------

QRMI is the work of `many contributors`_ from across the quantum and HPC communities.

If you use QRMI in your research, please cite the overview paper,
`Quantum Resources in Resource Management Systems`_. Citations help
support the continued development, visibility, and adoption of the project.

BibTeX citation information is available in the `QRMI citation file`_.

.. note::

   As the project evolves, updated versions of the overview paper may be
   released. When possible, cite the most recent version relevant to your work.

Quantum-HPC Integration Paper
-----------------------------

For work focused on integration between quantum resources and HPC systems,
you may also wish to cite

.. _qrmi_overview:

QRMI Overview
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

QRMI is the work of `many people`_ who contribute to the project at different levels.
If you use QRMI, please consider citing the associated overview paper,
`"Quantum resources in resource management systems"`_. This helps support the continued
development and visibility of the repository. 

The BibTeX citation handle can be found in the `BibTeX file`_.

.. _many people: https://github.com/qiskit-community/qrmi/graphs/contributors
.. _"Quantum resources in resource management systems": https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10052
.. _BibTeX file: https://github.com/qiskit-community/qrmi/blob/main/CITATION.bib

.. note::

    We expect multiple versions of the overview paper to be released as the project evolves.
